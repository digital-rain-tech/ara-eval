## Jurisdiction Context — Singapore

Consider Singapore-specific regulatory frameworks:
- MAS FEAT Principles (Nov 2018) and the Veritas initiative for AI/data analytics in financial services
- MAS Information Paper on AI Model Risk Management (Dec 2024) and the proposed MAS Guidelines on AI Risk Management (consultation, Nov 2025)
- PDPC Advisory Guidelines on Use of Personal Data in AI Recommendation and Decision Systems (Mar 2024) and the PDPA
- MAS Guidelines on Individual Accountability and Conduct (IAC, effective Sep 2021) for who is accountable
- Securities and Futures Act (SFA) for capital markets products; Payment Services Act (PSA) for digital payment token services
- MAS stablecoin regulatory framework (Aug 2023); Project Guardian / Global Layer One for asset tokenisation

Note: unlike some jurisdictions, Singapore imposes **no blanket statutory prohibition on fully autonomous AI decision-making**. Human oversight is recommended/expected for high-impact decisions (FEAT, PDPC, MAS AI papers) but is not an absolute legal bar. Regulatory exposure derives chiefly from the **underlying regulated activity** (e.g., a securities offer under the SFA, a cross-border transfer under PDPA s.26), not from a standalone "AI must have a human" rule.
